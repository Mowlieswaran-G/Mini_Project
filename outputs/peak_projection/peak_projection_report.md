### 4. Technical Architecture and Validation
- **Model Loaded:** Trained baseline Linear Regression (`linear_regression_delhi_powerdemand.pkl`).
- **Model Training State:** **Zero Retraining**; original weights and biases strictly preserved.
- **Feature Mapping:** All 17 features (`year`, `month`, `day`, `hour`, `minute`, `day_of_week`, `is_weekend`, 4 exact historical lags, and 6 weather features) arranged in identical schema.

### 5. Limitations of Scenario Projections
1. **Autoregressive Dependency:** The model relies heavily on `demand_lag_5min` ($w pprox 1.046$). When projecting consecutive multi-hour sequences into the distant future without real-time SCADA telemetry, simulated autoregressive recursive feedback can accumulate drift.
2. **Linear Weather Sensitivity:** Linear Regression models temperature with a constant linear slope ($w_{temp} pprox +0.395$ MW/°C), which does not fully capture the exponential 'hockey stick' non-linearity observed when Delhi temperatures exceed 42 °C–45 °C.
3. **Exogenous Accuracy:** Projection reliability is fundamentally bounded by the quality and accuracy of upstream meteorological forecasts provided to the model.
